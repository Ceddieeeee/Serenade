A FEW MORE WORDS FROM MY HEART

To My Wifeyyy,
My Favorite Person

My love,

I don't think I'll ever find enough words to explain just how much you mean to me, but I'll always try, because you deserve to know how deeply you are loved.

Sometimes, I find myself thinking about how different my life was before you came into it. I never really expected to meet someone who would make me feel this way. I was so used to being on my own, keeping my emotions to myself, and convincing myself that I didn't need anyone. I never imagined that one person could make me look at life so differently.

But here you are.

And I wouldn't trade what we have for anything.

I want you to know that I appreciate you, not only for the things you do for me, but simply for being you. Your existence has brought so much happiness into my life. Even the smallest things about you can make my day better. Your smile, your laughter, the way you talk, your little habits, and all the things that make you who you are. I love them more than you probably realize.

I hope you never feel that you have to be perfect around me. You don't have to hide your sadness, pretend to be okay, or force yourself to be strong all the time. I want to be someone you can lean on, someone you can be vulnerable with, and someone who will listen even when you don't know how to explain what you're feeling.

I want to celebrate your happiest moments, comfort you during your hardest days, support your dreams, and remind you of your worth whenever you forget it.

And my love, I want a future with you.

I want to experience all the little things with you. I want to travel to places we've only dreamed of visiting, discover things we've never tried, take pictures of moments we never want to forget, and accomplish the dreams we've been working so hard for. I want us to grow, learn, make mistakes, and become better people together.

I know our journey won't always be easy. There will be days when things don't go according to plan, moments when we misunderstand each other, and challenges we never expected. But I hope that no matter what life brings, we continue choosing to understand each other, respect each other, and never stop making an effort.

I don't need a perfect love story. I just want a real one with you.

I want the ordinary days, the spontaneous adventures, the quiet nights, the silly conversations, the little arguments we eventually laugh about, and all the beautiful moments we haven't experienced yet.

If there's one thing I want you to remember, it's this: you are not someone I want only for the beautiful moments. You're someone I want beside me through every season of life.

Thank you for letting me into your world. Thank you for trusting me, for loving me, and for giving me a reason to look forward to all the things that are yet to come.

I may not know exactly what tomorrow holds for us, but I know that I want to keep choosing you through every tomorrow that comes.

And if I could live this life all over again, knowing that somewhere along the way I would meet you, I'd still choose the same path, just to find my way back to you.

I love you, My Wifeyyy. More than these words could ever explain.

Here's to our whole new world, and to all the beautiful chapters we have yet to write together.

Your Hubby. ❤️
